## What it is

PE Pro is a mobile-first app for PE teachers. It brings lesson planning, professional development, video resources, assessments and reporting features into one place, so users can plan, learn and gather evidence without stitching together separate tools.

It is also one of Russell Tech’s own products, which means the work covered the full journey from identifying the need to shaping, building and improving the product.

## The challenge

The challenge was not simply to add more features. PE professionals needed an app that could present a lot of useful material without becoming difficult to navigate. The experience had to support different kinds of work — planning, learning, recording and reporting — while remaining clear enough to use day to day.

## How Russell Tech built it

Russell Tech worked across product thinking, interface design, app development and the supporting technical foundations. The application follows a cross-platform approach, giving users one coherent product experience across the places they need to use it rather than maintaining separate mobile products with different behaviour.

The build was organised around the main jobs users return to: finding resources, preparing lessons, recording activity and returning to evidence later. That kept the app connected to the workflow instead of becoming a long list of disconnected screens. The educational material and resources are part of the product; Russell Tech’s role was designing and building the app that brings them together.

## What we worked on

- Product direction and feature shaping
- Mobile-first interface design
- Cross-platform app development
- Features for lesson planning and professional development
- Video resource, assessment and reporting features
- Ongoing improvements and support

## The result

PE Pro gives PE professionals a single, more coherent place to plan, learn and gather evidence. It shows how Russell Tech approaches app development: start with a real working problem, keep the product understandable and build the technical foundations to support it properly.
