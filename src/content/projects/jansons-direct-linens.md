## What it is

Jansons Direct Linens is a customer-facing e-commerce website for a business whose products need to be understood before someone is ready to buy or enquire. The website has to do more than show a catalogue: it needs to present the range clearly, answer the obvious questions and make the next step feel straightforward.

## The challenge

The existing challenge was one of clarity and confidence. Customers needed to browse the range without getting lost, understand what was relevant to them and move naturally towards an enquiry. That meant treating the website as part of the sales journey rather than as a collection of product pages.

## How Russell Tech built it

Russell Tech worked across the structure, presentation and responsive implementation. The build was shaped around a simple customer journey: introduce the offer, make products easy to compare and keep the route to contact visible on both desktop and mobile.

The technical approach was deliberately practical. The site needed to load reliably, work well across screen sizes and give the business a foundation it could continue to use, without adding complexity for its own sake.

## What we worked on

- Customer-facing website structure and page journeys
- E-commerce presentation and product browsing
- Responsive layouts for mobile and desktop
- Clear calls to action and enquiry routes
- Performance-minded website development

## The result

Jansons Direct Linens has a clearer digital front door: customers can understand the offer more quickly and move towards an enquiry with less friction. It is a useful business tool built around the way people actually decide whether to get in touch.
