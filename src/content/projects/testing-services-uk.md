## What it is

Testing Services UK is a specialist website for an organisation providing electrical and fire inspection, testing and installation services. It acts as both a public-facing explanation of the business and an internal publishing tool for keeping that information current.

## The challenge

Specialist services can be difficult to explain online. Customers need enough detail to understand what is offered and what may be relevant to them, while the team running the business needs to update pages without working around a generic website structure.

## How Russell Tech built it

Russell Tech designed and built a custom website and CMS around the organisation’s actual content and day-to-day publishing needs. The site structure turns a broad set of specialist services into a clearer journey, with room for useful information and trust-building detail.

The custom CMS is an important part of the build. Rather than treating content management as an afterthought, the publishing workflow was shaped alongside the public website so the team could keep service pages and supporting information up to date themselves.

## What we worked on

- Website structure and service journeys
- Custom CMS development
- Specialist service content architecture
- Trust-building and explanatory content
- Responsive website development
- A maintainable publishing workflow

## The result

Testing Services UK has a more useful digital front door for customers looking for specialist testing and installation services. Behind it, the custom CMS gives the team a practical way to manage the site as the organisation’s services and information develop.
